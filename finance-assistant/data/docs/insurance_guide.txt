Insurance Guide for Indian Salaried Individuals

Term Life Insurance:
Pure protection — no investment component. Best value for money.
Coverage: 10-15x annual income. For ₹10L salary: ₹1-1.5 crore coverage.
Premium for ₹1 crore cover: ₹8,000-15,000/year (30-year-old, non-smoker, 30-year term).
Best providers: LIC e-term, HDFC Click2Protect, ICICI iProtect Smart.
Premium qualifies for Section 80C deduction up to ₹1.5L.

Health Insurance:
Individual: minimum ₹5L cover. Family floater: ₹10-15L recommended.
Premium deductible under Section 80D: ₹25,000/year (₹50,000 if 60+).
Top plans: Star Health, Niva Bupa, HDFC Ergo, Care Health.
Always check: room rent limit, co-payment, pre-existing disease waiting period, network hospitals.
Super top-up plans: cost-effective way to increase health cover after base limit.

Critical Illness Insurance:
Lump-sum payout on diagnosis of specified critical illnesses (cancer, heart attack, stroke).
Coverage: ₹25-50L recommended.
Premium: ₹5,000-10,000/year for ₹50L cover (healthy 35-year-old).

What NOT to buy:
- ULIP (Unit Linked Insurance Plan): expensive charges eat into returns. Keep insurance and investment separate.
- Endowment/Money-back policies: very low returns (4-6%), use term + mutual funds instead.
- Accidental death benefit riders: usually not worth the extra premium; better to increase term cover.

Insurance needs checklist:
✓ Term insurance: ✓ if married/have dependants
✓ Health insurance: ✓ always (even if employer provides — supplement with personal policy)
✓ Critical illness: ✓ if family history of serious illness
✓ Vehicle insurance: legally mandatory (third-party at minimum)
