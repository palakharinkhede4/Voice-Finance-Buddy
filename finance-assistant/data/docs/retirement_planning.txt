Retirement Planning in India — Complete Guide

How much do you need for retirement?
Use the rule: Monthly Expenses × 12 × 25 (the 4% withdrawal rule).
For example: ₹50,000/month expenses → ₹50,000 × 12 × 25 = ₹1.5 crore retirement corpus.
Always account for inflation: money worth ₹1L today will need ₹2.19L in 20 years at 4% inflation.

Best retirement instruments in India:
1. NPS (National Pension System) — market-linked, tax benefit under 80CCD(1B) ₹50,000 extra.
2. PPF — 7.1% tax-free, but only ₹1.5L/year limit.
3. EPF (Employee Provident Fund) — 8.15% tax-free for salaried employees. Mandatory above ₹15,000 salary.
4. Equity Mutual Funds via SIP — historically 12-15% CAGR over 15+ years.
5. Retirement Mutual Funds — specifically structured for long-term retirement corpus.

Retirement planning milestones:
- 20s: Invest 10-15% of income. Start early — compounding works best over time.
- 30s: Increase SIP, ensure 6-month emergency fund, get term insurance.
- 40s: Shift 20-30% to debt/balanced funds, maximize NPS contributions.
- 50s: Capital preservation mode, reduce equity to 40-50%, plan annuity purchase.

EPF Details:
Employee contributes 12% of basic salary; employer matches 12% (8.33% goes to EPS pension scheme).
Interest rate: 8.15% for FY 2023-24 (tax-free if held for 5+ years).
Withdrawal rules: Full withdrawal at retirement (58 years) or after 2 months of unemployment.
